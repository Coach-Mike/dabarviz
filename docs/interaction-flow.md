# DabarViz — Interaction Flow

## Primary Loop

1. **Entry**  
   User lands on the Hybrid view:  
   - Top: Rainbow Arc  
   - Middle: Theme Graph  
   - Bottom: Biblical Globe  
   - Left: Passage panel  
   - Right: Evidence / Word Study panel

2. **Discover**  
   - Search (verse, theme, person, place, Hebrew/Greek word)  
   - Or click/brush a region of the Arc

3. **Synchronize**  
   Selecting any element updates all layers:  
   - Arc highlights related connections  
   - Graph expands or focuses relevant nodes  
   - Globe lights matching locations  
   - Left panel loads English + original language  
   - Right panel shows connection evidence or word study

4. **Drill**  
   - Click a word in the original-language view → Word Study panel (root tree, semantic range, every occurrence, cultural notes)  
   - Click a connection → Evidence panel (type of link, shared language, manuscript support, confidence)

5. **Trail**  
   Pin the current path (verse → theme → word → place) and share it.

6. **Layers**  
   Independently toggle: Text, Morphology, Themes, Geography, History, Evidence.

## Genesis 1 ↔ John 1 Example Flow

1. User searches “Genesis 1:1” or clicks the beginning of the Arc.
2. Arc highlights the long arc to John 1.
3. Graph focuses on “Creation / Logos / Word” node.
4. Globe remains contextual (no specific journey yet).
5. Left panel shows Genesis 1:1 in English + Hebrew (with morphology).
6. Clicking בְּרֵאשִׁית or דָּבָר-related terms opens Word Study.
7. Right panel shows the intertextual evidence for the John 1 connection.
