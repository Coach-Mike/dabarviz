# Genesis 1:1 — English + Original Language

## English (ESV)
In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.

## Hebrew (Masoretic Text)
בְּרֵאשִׁית בָּרָא אֱלֹהִים אֵת הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֵת הָאָרֶץ

## Key Word Notes

- **בְּרֵאשִׁית (bĕrē’šît)**: “In the beginning.” Not a temporal point only; it marks the absolute start of God’s creative activity.
- **בָּרָא (bārā’)**: Used only of God’s creative activity in the Hebrew Bible. Distinct from עָשָׂה (“make/do”).
- **אֱלֹהִים (’ĕlōhîm)**: Plural form used with singular verbs — the one God who speaks and creates.

## John 1:1 Parallel (Greek)

ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος…  
En archē ēn ho logos…  
“In the beginning was the Word…”

The Greek deliberately echoes the Hebrew opening.
